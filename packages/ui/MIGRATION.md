## erxes-ui 3.x and ui-modules exports

What a 3.x plugin imported, and what it imports now. The record pickers read core through the
query options of `@erxes/core-contract/client` (`unitQueries`, `productQueries`,
`contactSearchQueries`, `memberQueries`), passed as a prop, as `SelectMember` already took
`members`. Every picker family (`SelectBranches`, `SelectDepartments`, `SelectProduct`,
`SelectCategory`, `SelectCustomer`, `SelectCompany`, `SelectMember`) has `InlineCell`, `FormItem`,
`Field`, `FilterItem`, `FilterView`, `FilterBar`, `Content` and `Value` with 3.x's `value`,
`onValueChange` and `mode` (`single` or `multiple`); a filter part keeps the id, or the ids
comma-separated, under `filterKey` (nuqs `parseAsString` or `parseAsArrayOf(parseAsString)`).

| 3.x                                                                                                                  | Now                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

| `CurrencyField` (`ValueInput`, `SelectCurrency`, `SelectCurrencyCommand`), `CURRENCY_CODES`, `CurrencyCode`          | the same; `currencies` is a list of codes (`organization.currencies`), not a record of `CURRENCY_CODES` entries; `CurrencyCode.MNT` is `'MNT'`; `isCurrencyCode` narrows a stored code; `CURRENCY_CODES` (each entry's `icon` a Tabler icon name for `IconComponent`) is imported from `erxes-ui/constants/CurrencyCodes`, off the shared chunk |
